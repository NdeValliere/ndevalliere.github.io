Caveat on panel 3: feTurbulence at these sub-pixel base frequencies is rendered at slightly different scales by different browsers, so the grain is not guaranteed identical across Safari, Chrome and Firefox. If you need it pinned down, rasterise the noise once to a small tiling PNG — a 128×128 tile is about 15 KB, since random noise barely compresses.
